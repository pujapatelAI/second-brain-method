Questions

Will this work for my kind of business?

If you do $50k to $250k a month and your team does the same jobs every week, yes. The audit shows which of your jobs to hand over first.

Is this more work for my team?

A little at the start, then less. One person on your team owns it. The goal is to take work off your team's list.
