How do I set up AI correctly as a non-technical business owner?

Three things, before any AI tool: map how your business runs as workflows (trigger, steps, completion condition, owner, input, output), get the information in your head into files your AI can read, and pick one tool as the hub. Then you do your work through that one tool instead of six open tabs.

Questions this video answers

Do I need to know how to code, or know anything about AI?
No. The setup is about operations and data: mapping how the business runs, getting the information out of your head into files, and working through one tool. You don't need to try every AI tool or tip you see online.
What has to be in place before I pick an AI tool?
Your data and your systems. Map how customers are acquired and how the service is delivered as workflows, each with a trigger, the steps, a completion condition, an owner, an input and an output. Most of the problems come from there, not from a lack of AI.
Which tool should be the hub?
One tool, never two with the same function. Puja recommends Claude Code; Codex or an equivalent works too. Use the code version, not the chat version, and connect your other tools to it.
What does the day to day look like after the setup?
One tool open. You ask for the work ("write a proposal for Jen") and it reads your email, your meeting transcripts and the memory you built, then drafts it in your structure, instead of you opening six tabs and doing it by hand.
