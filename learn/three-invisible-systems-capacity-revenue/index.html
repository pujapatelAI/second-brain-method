Questions this video answers

How does the morning project management brief work without a project management tool?
Her second brain in Claude has all her data, tools and projects. Every morning it checks her calendar, sees how many hours she has to work that day, and gives her the tasks that fit those hours and move the projects toward the end goal. She doesn't open anything or think about it, and she doesn't miss deadlines or updates.
What does the client health system check?
For her consulting clients and her group program, it checks what is expected of her under the contract, what is done and what is left, whether she has been hearing back from people, whether payments are good, and whether projects are moving forward every week. Anyone below a certain threshold gets extra attention that week, so there is no churn.
Does lead follow-up need AI?
No. It is basic automation, because the sequence is known and there is no nuance. New TikTok followers get an automatic message, and Meta Ads leads get email sequences, text messages and sales call reminders without her. She says it increases show rates and closes.
