About

I'm Puja.

I help business owners get their business out of their own heads. I learned how by doing it to mine first.

I work where operations, data and AI meet. I have a degree in data science and seven years at that intersection: three of them running day-to-day operations for a multi-property portfolio, where I first put AI into daily workflows, then standing up the processes and systems of a $70 million solar manufacturing facility from scratch.

For the last two years I have done this work for clients across retail, brokerage, recruiting, real estate, events and healthcare. The pattern is almost always the same: the whole thing runs on a few people's heads, the numbers live in five places, and the AI tools they pay for touch neither.

My own business runs on a second brain I built for myself first. Clients, pipeline, content, cash. It is what lets me run everything without being the bottleneck in it.
