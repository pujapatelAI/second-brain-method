What's a second brain?

Your business runs on what's in your head — how you price, who does what, your standards, the way you talk to clients. A second brain gets all of that out of your head and into one place your AI can actually read and run.

So the work stops routing through you, and your AI stops guessing — it operates from how your business really works. That's the difference between AI that just impresses you and AI that actually runs things.

Questions

What people ask before they join.

Is my data safe? I'm not comfortable giving AI access to my business.
That instinct is the right one, and you stay in control of it. Claude only sees what you choose to give it. Start with one folder of the things you're comfortable sharing, and that's all it can read. You can widen it when you're ready. Most people start small.
How much time does this take each week?
About 30 minutes a day, one small task at a time. Plus the live sessions when you can make them. The workshops are recorded, so you can catch up if you miss one.
Do I need to pay for anything else?
You'll need your own Claude account by your first build. That's billed by Anthropic, separate from your membership. You don't need it to get started.
What exactly do I get?
OpMap, which maps your business, analyzes your workflows and builds a 30-day plan around your bottlenecks. Plus a monthly deep-dive workshop, one live build a month, a working session every week, and the operator community.
What if I get stuck?
That's what the weekly working sessions are for. Bring what you're stuck on and leave with it moving.
What happens to my work if I cancel?
Everything you build lives in your own files and accounts, so it stays yours. Cancel anytime from your account, no call needed. OpMap access ends when your membership does.
