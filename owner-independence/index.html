How it works

Four things have to be true to pass the test. We build all four in 120 days.

Most companies don't fail the 14-Day Owner Absence Test because the team is weak. They fail because the owner's decisions don't live anywhere except the owner's head.

So the program doesn't add people or meetings. It moves your decisions out of your head and into a structure your managers can run: who decides what, written down, with systems carrying the routine work. Then, at the end, you take the test.

What owners ask before the call.

What is the 14-Day Owner Absence Test?
For fourteen days, you step out of your business. You don't approve anything routine, you don't sit in an operations meeting, and nobody texts you a quick question. The only things that reach you are the few decisions we agreed, in writing, are still yours. If the agreed numbers hold and your team makes the calls, your company passes.
What happens if my company doesn't pass?
I keep working. If your company fails the test we agreed on up front, and your team did its part, you get up to 60 more days of implementation with no additional fee for my work. The terms are written into the agreement before we start.
What does it cost?
Packages start at $100,000. The scope is set to your company, and we go through it on the call. It is a one-time build, and your team keeps everything.
How much of my time does it take?
Less than your managers'. Your part is heaviest at the start, while we get your decisions out of your head. After that, most of the work happens with your managers. That's on purpose.
Wouldn't hiring a COO do the same thing?
A COO walks into the same structure. Your decisions still live in your head, so now they ask you, or they guess. This builds the structure first, so the managers you have, or a COO you hire later, can actually run the company.
Nobody else can make my calls. How would my team?
Your calls are more consistent than you think. When we write down the decisions you've already made, most of them turn into rules your team can follow. In the accounting firm I mapped, 43 of the 86 times the partners stepped in, the answer already existed in writing.
I'd like to sell someday. Does this matter?
A lot. Value Builder's data on tens of thousands of businesses found that companies able to run for three months without their owner were more than twice as likely to get a premium offer, more than six times profit. A company that needs you is worth less, to a buyer and to you.
What happens on the call?
45 minutes with me. We look at where your company routes through you and what it would take to pass the test. If it's a fit, we talk scope. If it's not, I'll tell you. Nothing gets mapped on the call. That is the first part of the paid work.
Is our data safe?
Everything is built inside approved, enterprise-grade, data-isolated tools where your data is not used to train anyone's model. Where client or patient data is involved, it is built to that compliance standard from the start.
