Straight answers

Before you book.

What does it cost? +

Is the audit separate from the build? +
Yes — they're two different engagements. The audit comes first: we map your operation and hand you the ranked build plan, and it's a standalone deliverable you keep even if you go no further. The build is a second engagement, scoped from what the audit found — and if you move forward, your audit credits toward it. The free 30-minute call is neither; it's just where we see if there's a fit.
How is this different from hiring a person or buying software? +
You're not buying a tool or a headcount. We rebuild your operation and run it on AI — so the work gets done without another salary and without you having to learn a new platform cold. At the end, your team runs it.
Will it work with my current stack? +
Yes — we build on top of the tools you already use, LOS and CRM included. It's not a rip-and-replace; the goal is your existing pipeline running smoother, with the re-keying and doc-chasing gone.
Do you handle residential, hard money, and commercial? +
Yes. Each loan type keeps its own path — residential, private / hard money, and commercial all underwrite differently — and we template each one so the busywork is gone without forcing every file through the same box.
How long does it take, and how much of my time? +
It's done for you, so your time is mostly reviewing as we go rather than building. We'll walk through the timeline on the call once we've seen the scope.
