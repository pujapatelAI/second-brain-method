The questions firms ask first

Built to work inside your rules — not around them.

Does our confidential client data go into public AI?
No. Everything is built inside approved, enterprise-grade, data-isolated tools — the kind your IT team already vets, where your data isn't used to train anyone's model. Confidential project files, drawings, and contracts never touch a public service. Governance is the second layer we install, not an afterthought.
We can only use tools that pass IT / security review. Does that work?
Yes — I build inside your approved-tool stack and your data-classification rules, alongside your IT and internal champions, never around them. If a tool needs review first, I support that process on your timeline.
We're accountable for our work. What about AI mistakes?
The system is built so a person always reviews before anything ships. AI drafts; your professionals decide. Nothing auto-submits to a client or a regulator — the human checkpoint is designed in, not bolted on.
We're cautious about autonomous ("agentic") AI. Are you turning bots loose?
No. We build the foundation first. Anything autonomous comes later — only with human checkpoints, limited data scope, logging, and a rollback — inside your governance, at your pace. The foundation is what makes safe automation possible at all.
Who owns what we build? Any IP risk?
You own all of it — it's your system, on your accounts. We document the tools and approach so your AI-assisted work stays defensible and yours.
Do we need to be technical? None of us can code.
No — and that's the point. Your team's expertise is your business, not software. We build the company brain with your team, in plain language, using your real work — no coding, no setup homework. If you can explain how your firm runs, you can build this. It lives in tools your team already knows how to click around in.
How does this fit our required AI training?
The Build Day is hands-on training — your team learns by building their own system into their real work, and keeps it. It slots into your training cadence, and an internal champion carries it forward after we leave.
What if a client contract restricts AI on their project?
The system respects per-project boundaries — you control what runs where, so a restricted project stays restricted while the rest of the firm keeps moving.
